# Product

## Register

product

## Users

This is a single-user English listening practice tool. The user studies independently on a desktop, often for sustained sessions, and needs to move from subtitle-dependent listening toward understanding spoken English without subtitles.

## Product Purpose

EnglishPod organizes existing lessons, subtitles, sentence lookup, vocabulary, and spaced review into a focused listening workflow. Success means the user can concentrate on the audio, understand more without subtitles, retain useful expressions, and complete daily practice without unnecessary setup or interface friction.

## Brand Personality

Focused, simple, fluid. The interface should feel calm and dependable, keep attention on the lesson, and make the next useful action obvious without feeling instructional or heavy-handed.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a gamified vocabulary app, a complex learning-management platform, a social product, or a flashy AI assistant. Avoid rankings, coins, badges, decorative dashboards, dense configuration, unnecessary cards, and competing calls to action.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the lesson content at the visual center; guidance supports it without competing with it.
2. Make the current listening task and next action immediately clear.
3. Prefer one direct workflow over configurable or platform-like complexity.
4. Preserve user control through a free-listening mode without weakening the guided default.
5. Use space efficiently so sustained desktop study feels calm rather than vertically cramped.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use comfortably readable text sizes and strong contrast for long study sessions. Avoid relying on color alone for state, keep keyboard-operable controls and visible focus states, and use restrained motion with reduced-motion support where animation is present.
